    ⛔ Makrooh auqaat (is mein namaz nahi parhte)

    Ye auqaat taqreeban hain. Pakki tasdeeq ke liye apne mustanad aalim ya maqami masjid ke kalendar se zaroor dekh lein.

    ⚙️ Hisaab ka tareeqa

    Auqaat phone mein hisaab se nikalte hain, is liye internet ke baghair bhi chalte hain. Maqami masjid ke waqt se 1 se 3 minute ka farq ho sakta hai.

    1. Phone haath mein pakdein.
